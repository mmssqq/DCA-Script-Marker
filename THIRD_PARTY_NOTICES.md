## MuPDF components and resources

The MuPDF 1.27.2 source distribution includes third-party libraries and
resources with their own notices, including FreeType, HarfBuzz, libjpeg,
Little CMS, OpenJPEG, zlib, Gumbo, Brotli, jbig2dec, MuJS, and embedded font,
CMap, and hyphenation resources. Depending on build options it can also include
FreeGLUT, curl, JPEG-XR, Tesseract, Leptonica, Zint, and ZXing-C++.

The complete, authoritative notices are retained in the unmodified
`mupdf-1.27.2-source.tar.gz` archive included with the matching source release.
See the upstream [MuPDF third-party component list](https://mupdf.readthedocs.io/en/1.27.2/other/third-party.html).
